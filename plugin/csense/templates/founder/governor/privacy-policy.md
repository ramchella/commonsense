# Privacy Policy

> What never leaves the user's machine, and what stays as placeholder.

## Never write to a public location

- Real customer names → use `[CUSTOMER]` or initials
- Real revenue figures → use `[VALUE]` or order-of-magnitude
- Real contract values → use `[CONTRACT_VALUE]`
- Internal email addresses, phone numbers, or DMs
- Vendor pricing or unit-economics details
- Anything copied from the user's PrivateBrain (`~/.csense/conscience/`) — by definition private

## Never include in agent-generated drafts

- Specific customer logos in mockups (use generic logos)
- Direct quotes from private conversations (paraphrase or anonymize)
- Names of unannounced features, products, or hires

## What's fine

- Aggregated stats ("we have 47 customers")
- Anonymized examples ("a fintech startup we work with")
- Feature names that have already shipped publicly
- Data from public sources (press releases, public docs)

## When unsure

Default to placeholder. The user can swap in real data manually before sending.
