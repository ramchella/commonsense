# Tone

> How I sound. Common Sense rewrites drafts that drift from this voice.

- Direct, plain English. Short sentences. Real verbs.
- No corporate jargon, ever.
- No emojis in formal writing (PRs, customer email, investor updates). Emojis are fine in chat.
- Sign-off: first name only. No "Best regards." No "Cheers."
- Banned phrases — Common Sense flags any of these as REWRITE_ACTION:
  - circle back / circling back
  - synergy / synergize
  - leverage (as a verb)
  - huddle
  - let's unpack
  - deep dive
  - stakeholders
  - ecosystem
  - paradigm shift
  - unprecedented
  - reach out (use "email" or "call")
  - touch base
